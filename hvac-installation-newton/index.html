What to discuss before installation

A contractor conversation can cover the existing system, the areas you want to heat or cool, and the equipment being considered. Ask what information is needed to assess the home and how the proposed setup relates to your comfort goals. If you have concerns about particular rooms or the way you use the space, point them out rather than assuming they’ll be obvious.

Before agreeing to work, make sure you understand the proposed scope. Ask which parts of the system are included, what preparation you should do, and what may require a separate decision. Write down the answers. It’s easy to forget details when you’re comparing equipment or coordinating home plans.

A common mistake is focusing only on the equipment name. Another is expecting a contractor to know about comfort problems you haven’t mentioned. Share your observations, ask questions in plain language, and take time to review the recommendation.
